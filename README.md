SmartWasteAI-Intelligent- Recycling- Assistant 

Final project for the Building AI course

Summary

SmartWasteAI is an AI-driven solution that identifies and classifies recyclable materials using image recognition. The system helps households and companies sort waste correctly, reducing contamination and improving recycling efficiency.

Background

Which problems does your idea solve? How common or frequent is this problem? What is your personal motivation? Why is this topic important or interesting?

Improperly sorted recyclables lead to contamination, higher processing costs, and environmental impact.

Industrial recycling facilities face significant inefficiencies due to mixed materials.

In Germany alone, nearly 40% of packaging waste ends up in the wrong bin.

Personal motivation: working in paper production, I see daily how incorrect sorting reduces recycling quality.

Importance: Enhancing recycling efficiency supports sustainability, reduces CO₂ emissions, and promotes a circular economy.

How is it used?

Describe the process of using the solution. In what kind situations is the solution needed (environment, time, etc.)? Who are the users, what kinds of needs should be taken into account?

Households scan items using a smartphone or smart bin camera; the AI identifies the material and suggests the correct recycling bin.

Industrial recycling facilities can integrate SmartWasteAI into conveyor belts to automatically sort large volumes of waste.

Municipalities could deploy smart recycling bins in public spaces.

Users: Individuals, recycling operators, municipal authorities. Needs include accurate classification, user-friendly interface, and privacy protection.

Self-collected images from household and industrial settings.

AI Technique	Description
Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)	Image recognition and classification
Transfer Learning	Reduce training data requirements using pre-trained models
OCR (optional)	Read labels and recycling symbols for accurate sorting
Challenges

What does your project not solve? Which limitations and ethical considerations should be taken into account when deploying a solution like this?

Edge cases: items with mixed materials or damaged packaging.

Users must trust AI decisions; transparency is key.

Data collection must ensure privacy, especially for household cameras.

The system cannot completely replace human oversight in complex industrial environments.

How could your project grow and become something even more? What kind of skills, what kind of assistance would you need to move on?
